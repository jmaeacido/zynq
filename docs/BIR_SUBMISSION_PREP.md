# BIR Submission Preparation

ZYNQ is BIR-ready, not automatically BIR-approved. Final submission requirements must be verified with the client CPA, BIR RDO, and current BIR regulations.

## System Features to Demonstrate

- Tenant and branch configuration.
- Terminal MIN, PTU, serial number, accreditation, and software version.
- Invoice preview and invoice print pages.
- Sequential invoice numbering.
- VAT and NON-VAT reporting.
- Cash session opening and closing.
- X-reading and Z-reading.
- Void/refund approval and ledger entries.
- Audit trail.
- Disabled tenant behavior.

## Important Disclaimer

The software provides BIR-ready documentation, reports, audit trail, invoice templates, and compliance artifacts. It is not automatically BIR-approved and does not replace CPA, RDO, or BIR review.
